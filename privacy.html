Chapterwren — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 28 September 2026 · applies to the iPhone and Android apps

The short version: Chapterwren collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The photos you take, the words read from them and the reading log all stay on your phone, and reading happens on the phone too. Nothing your family does in this app is sent to us or to anyone else.

1. Who makes Chapterwren

Chapterwren is made by DAPS, LLC, a small United States company. There are no investors, no advertising and no ad network.

2. What the app does with the pages you photograph

When you photograph pages or pick photos, the app saves a copy of each page inside its own private storage on your phone. It finds the words on each page and reads them aloud on the phone itself, using the text-recognition and speech features built into the phone. The pages, the recognized words, your per-book settings (voice, speed, word fixes) and your place in each book never leave the phone. Deleting a book deletes its photos; deleting the app deletes everything.

The app has no way to export or share the pages or the audio, and it never records sound.

3. What we collect — the full list

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No name, email, age, birthdate, contacts, location or account of any kind.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no trackers.
  • The "Grown-ups" reading log (which days the app was used, for how long) is stored only on your phone. It leaves the phone only if you choose to copy it and paste it somewhere yourself.

4. Network

The app makes no network connections of its own. On Android it does not even have permission to use the internet. Two things your phone's own system software may do, outside the app:

  • Android page scanning: the page scanner is provided by Google Play services. The first time you use it, Google Play services may download the scanner to your phone; after that, scanning runs on the phone. Google's handling of that is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Voices: if you download a better voice in your phone's settings, the phone downloads it from Apple or Google. The app itself only uses voices that work offline.

5. Camera

The camera is used only to photograph book pages, only when you tap Scan pages. On Android the scanner uses Google Play services' camera permission; the app itself asks for none.

6. Children

Chapterwren is used by children with a parent's help. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Any future in-app purchase will be behind a grown-ups check.
